How a set goes on

Ten seconds a nail, and the rest of the afternoon is yours.

  1. Find your fit

    Twelve sizes in every box. Match each nail dry before a drop of glue goes anywhere.

  2. Press and hold

    Cuticle first, then roll the nail down flat. Ten seconds of pressure is the whole technique.

  3. Wear, then keep

    Two to three weeks on. Lift them gently with oil and the set goes back in the box for next time.
